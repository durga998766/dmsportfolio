A cowboy-themed personal brand and case-study system, built as the home for the PMGX case study and published on Behance. Where the other three cards are product/UX work, this project is about visual identity and storytelling craft. [Expand with your real narrative for the theme choice.]

Sub-project — Medini

Medini is a luxury handloom saree brand visual-identity concept nested inside this system. It covers brand story, competitor analysis, brand positioning, keywords (heritage, craftsmanship, sustainability, cultural identity), a moodboard, logomark construction and variations, a colour system, and typography (Rufina + Josefin Sans). Deliverables include tags, packaging, storefront signage, business cards and social assets.
