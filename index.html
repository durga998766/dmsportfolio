About Me

I think my curiosity for design started long before I actually knew what design was. As a kid, I was drawn to movie posters, game graphics, animations, and all the little visual details people often overlook. I didn't just wonder how they looked — I wondered, "How did they make this?" and "Why does this feel this way?" That curiosity pushed me to experiment, learn, and keep creating. Over time, I realised design isn't just about making things look good; it's about understanding why something feels right.

What I Do

I take messy ideas, find what really matters, and turn them into products that feel simple, thoughtful, and easy to understand.
